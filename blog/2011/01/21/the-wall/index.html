The Wall

January 21, 2011 · Tips, Navel_gazing

Starting new projects always feels great.  Pure possibility, stretching out forever!  Think of all of the awesome things you’ll get to make!  This’ll be the best, well, whatever, because you won’t make any of the mistakes that other people have, or you’ll build something even better than your predecessors, by borrowing here, copying there, polishing everywhere.  

Then, reality sets in.  Oh, shit, I’m going to have to build what?  Where in the hell do I even start?  The whole list is just too damned big!  Where do I even start?

Start small, start anywhere, but please, just freakin’ start.
